**Live trigger preview**

- The trigger deadzone bars now fill with your real trigger pull as you press it, so you can see exactly where a pull crosses the ignored area.
- Their labels follow the selected controller: **LT/RT** on Xbox pads, **L2/R2** on PlayStation pads, and **ZL/ZR** on Nintendo Switch pads.
- The shaded deadzone width and percentage still update live with the sliders.
