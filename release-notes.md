**FrameForge 1.17.0: a professional overlay you can make your own**

- **New in-game overlay.** Rebuilt from scratch: a big FPS number with 1% low, frame time and a live FPS graph, then your graphics card, processor and memory with load bars. It now also shows GPU power, fan speed, clock speed, video memory, CPU power and RAM when your PC's sensors report them.
- **Four styles**: Panel (everything, grouped), List (one reading per line, like AMD's and MSI Afterburner's overlays), Bar (one slim strip) and Mini (the number and the essentials).
- **Customize it deeply, on the FPS limiter tab**: choose exactly which of 18 readings show, the accent colour (presets or any colour), number style, size, background transparency (down to text only), corner shape, six screen positions, distance from the edge and the show/hide shortcut. A live preview follows every change, and so does the real overlay.
- **The FPS counter switch moved to the FPS limiter tab**, together with the hardware readings switch and every overlay setting. Settings has a button that takes you there.
- A reading your PC doesn't report is left off the overlay rather than shown as a wrong number, and the tab tells you which free tool to download when FPS or temperatures are missing.
