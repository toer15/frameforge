**Live trigger percentages on the pad**

- The LT and RT labels on the controller drawing now show the live analog percentage, for example **LT 37%**, updating as you pull the trigger slowly.
- All trigger labels use it: LT/RT on Xbox pads, L2/R2 on PlayStation pads and ZL/ZR on Switch pads. Digital Switch triggers show 0% or 100%.
- The bright trigger marker now sits along the lower edge of the trigger, so the percentage stays readable while the marker follows the press.
- Everything else on the Controller tab is unchanged.
