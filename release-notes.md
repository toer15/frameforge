**Controller deadzone and cubic aim curve**

- The **Controller** tab now has a deadzone slider for the left stick and one for the right stick (0–40 % of full travel), so each stick can be tuned separately.
- A new **cubic aim curve** slider (0 % linear → 100 % full cubic) gives finer control near the centre while keeping full speed at the edge — the usual cubic aim feel.
- Every stick now shows a **yellow deadzone ring** on your controller drawing. Drag a slider and the ring grows or shrinks right away; with the deadzone at 0 % it disappears.
- The live sticks and the **Live input** numbers follow the saved deadzone and curve immediately, so you can feel the change while testing. The values are saved with your other FrameForge settings.
- FrameForge applies this to its own live reading of the pad. Windows has no system-wide controller deadzone, so enter the same numbers in your game or Steam Input to get the same aim in-game.
